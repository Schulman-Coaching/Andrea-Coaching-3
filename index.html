Your 6-Week Journey

Each week builds upon the previous, creating a comprehensive transformation

1

Introduction

Examine Codependency and Emotional Underdevelopment, explore childhood traits and key challenges from emotional oversight

2

Self-Worth

Investigate intrinsic value, build internal validation, and foster stable identity independent of external events

3

Limits

Redefine boundaries innovatively, learn practical self-preservation through internal and external limits

4

Authenticity

Understand your true identity, share it considerately, and embrace your full self in worldly interactions

5

Personal Nurturing

Go beyond routine self-care, heal unmet childhood needs, and highlight the role of desires in fostering abundance

6

Harmony

Emphasize spontaneity and vitality alongside restraint, discover ways to shift from turmoil to integrated existence

About Andrea Schulman

Educational Leader & Program Director

Andrea Schulman is an accomplished educational leader with over 15 years of experience in Jewish education, curriculum development, and youth programming. Currently serving as the Director of Women's Missions, Shidduchim, Parent Programming & Recruiting at Aish in Jerusalem, Israel.

With a Master's in Education and Bachelor's in Psychology from the City University of New York, Andrea brings both academic rigor and practical expertise to her diverse roles. She specializes in mentorship and training, demonstrating exceptional ability in relationship building with students, parents, and staff members.

As a Kallah teacher since 2018 and an adult educator, Andrea's proven track record includes organizing large-scale missions, recruiting and training educational staff, developing curricula, and creating meaningful virtual and in-person engagement opportunities.
